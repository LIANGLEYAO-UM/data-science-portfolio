## Project Showcase

### Education vs. Income & Working Hours
**Research Question:** How does education level impact annual salary and weekly working hours in New York State?

**Visualization:**
![Education Distribution](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LIANGLEYAO-UM/data-science-project/main/Activity7.06_treemap.png)
![Salary and Hours](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LIANGLEYAO-UM/data-science-project/main/Activity7.06_violin.png)

**Findings:** Individuals with a Bachelor's degree or higher earn significantly higher annual salaries, with a wider income distribution. However, weekly working hours remain relatively stable across education levels.
